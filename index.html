    FAQ

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No signup, no limits on the number of sites. It runs on free infrastructure, so please be patient if it is busy.

    How is this different from Screaming Frog?

    Screaming Frog is a powerful desktop crawler for SEO professionals. Scan300 is deliberately simpler: it runs in your browser, crawls a sample of up to 25 pages and turns the findings into a prioritised to-do list with fixes.

    Why did some checks say "not applicable"?

    Some checks only apply to certain sites — for example Product schema checks only run if you have products, and hreflang checks only if the site is multilingual. They do not affect your score.

    How is the score calculated?

    High-impact checks count 3 points, medium 2 and low 1. The score is the share of points you earn out of all checks that apply to your site.

    Do you store my data?

    No. The scan runs in your browser; pages are fetched through a small proxy that does not store anything. Nothing is saved after you close the tab.

    Can it measure Core Web Vitals?

    We check the things that cause slow pages (render-blocking scripts, image sizes, compression, caching, missing dimensions). For real-user Core Web Vitals, use Google PageSpeed Insights alongside this report.
